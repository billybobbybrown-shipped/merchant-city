
## A shipment that can never move now says so

A carrot farm was set to ship corn. Nothing was wrong with logistics — the
property simply never had the goods, so every tick moved nothing and there was
no way to tell that from the panel. The bay view now reports what the property
can actually put on a truck (`onHand` storage plus the `produces` of its field
or mine): the goods picker ranks those first and labels them ("Carrots —
produced here", "Planks — 40 here"), and an outbound line for goods the property
neither produces nor holds is tagged **never ships**.

## Gold ore and gold ingots

Two separate items, with a smelting step between them: a gold quarry produces
**gold ore** (base $95), and three ore smelt into one **gold ingot** (base $320)
over five minutes at a smelter — slower and lossier than iron's two-ore, two-
minute conversion, for a margin of $35 a bar. Ore renders as rough nuggets on a
shelf, the ingot as stacked poured bars, each with its own icon.
